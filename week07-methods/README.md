#### Participants
One participant (myself) will complete the experiment using a computer keyboard.

#### Materials
The experiment will use four colour words: RED, BLUE, GREEN, and YELLOW. Each word will appear in coloured ink. The response keys will be Z for red, X for blue, N for green, and M for yellow.

#### Procedure
The participant will identify the ink colour rather than read the word. Congruent trials will have matching words and ink colours, while incongruent trials will have different words and ink colours. A fixation cross will appear for 500 ms before each stimulus. There will be a 500 ms gap between trials. The experiment will include 8 practice trials followed by 40 scored trials (20 congruent and 20 incongruent), presented in randomized order. Each stimulus will remain visible until a response is made.

#### Data Handling
The experiment will record the condition, word, ink colour, response key, accuracy, and reaction time in milliseconds. Practice trials, incorrect responses, and reaction times below 150 ms or above 2500 ms will be excluded from the reaction-time analysis. Mean correct reaction times will be calculated separately for congruent and incongruent trials. The Stroop effect will be calculated as the incongruent mean minus the congruent mean.
