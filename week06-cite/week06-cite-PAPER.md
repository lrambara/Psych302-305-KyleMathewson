# Week 6 — Citation and Paper Summary

## Part 1: Paper Reference

**Reference:**

Pagoti, G. F., Hogan, J. A., & Willemart, R. H. (2024). Habituation to a predatory stimulus in a harvester (Arachnida, Opiliones). *Animal Cognition, 27*, Article 21. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10071-024-01857-7

**Summary:**

This study investigated habituation in harvestmen, which are arachnids, by examining how their defensive responses changed after repeated exposure to a simulated predator. The researchers wanted to understand whether the animals would gradually respond less strongly to a threatening stimulus that was presented repeatedly. The experiment focused on changes in defensive behaviour across repeated encounters. The study is relevant to learning psychology because habituation is a simple form of non-associative learning in which responses to repeated stimuli decrease. It also helps researchers understand how animals adjust their behaviour when encountering familiar threats.
