## Comparison and Reflection

The automated tool produced summaries much faster than writing them individually by hand. It identified relevant research questions and methods, but its limitations were sometimes general methodological critiques rather than limitations directly stated by the authors. I would keep the AI-generated summaries as starting points, but I would check the original papers before citing them in a literature review. I would trust a literature review based on verified original papers more than one based solely on AI-generated summaries.
